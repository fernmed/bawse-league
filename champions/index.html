        05The rules, short version

        Season-one ruleset — simplified by league vote in July 2026. This page is the current word; it supersedes the old rulebook docs.

        🐍

        One draft, then done

        12 managers × 11 rounds = 132 picks from 647 teams. Rosters lock when the draft ends — no trades, no waivers, no pickups, nothing.

        🏆

        Seven trophies pay

        The pot splits into 7 equal shares — World Series, NBA Finals, Stanley Cup, Super Bowl, CFP title, March Madness, Premier League. Own the champion, collect the share.

        🔁

        Unowned champions roll over

        Undrafted teams stay unowned. If one wins its title (hello, March Madness), that share rolls into next season's pot.

        ⏰

        Skips are forever

        Miss your pick clock on draft night and the draft moves on — you finish with one fewer team. The board remembers.

        📅

        The league year

        Draft night to the last trophy in June. There's a live championship race in every month of the season.

        🐐

        GOAT points

        Every Champions League title is worth 1 point in the five-year Bawse G.O.A.T. chapter race, alongside your redraft finishes. $300/$200/$100 at chapter's end.
