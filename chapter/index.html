      04How it works

      🏈

      Redraft finish pays

      Your final playoff finish in the Bawse League scores Bawse Points — 1st earns 12 BPs, down to 1 for 12th. Win the championship, bank the full 12.

      🏆

      Titles pay

      Every Champions League championship you own is worth 1 BP — seven are up for grabs every league year, and they add up fast.

      📈

      Five seasons deep

      The chapter runs 2026 through 2030. Consistency wins it — one great year won't, five good ones might.

      💰

      The podium

      $300 / $200 / $100 for the top three when the chapter closes in 2031. Bragging rights: permanent.

      🔒

      BPs lock at season's end

      In-season numbers here are live projections. A season's BPs go on the permanent record when its last trophy is decided.

      🔄

      Then we reset

      A chapter is a five-year commitment to a league idea. When it ends, the Chapter Champion is crowned and the next era begins.
